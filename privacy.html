TrialGuard — Privacy Policy and Terms

Last updated: 26 November 2026

TrialGuard helps you remember when free trials end and when subscription payments are due. Our promise: never get charged by surprise. This page explains what data TrialGuard uses and the rules for using it.

1. Who we are

TrialGuard is operated by Motez Souai ("we", "us"). Contact: msouai96@gmail.com.

2. Your data stays on your device

TrialGuard has no accounts and no sign-up. The subscriptions you enter (service name, date, amount, currency, billing frequency, notes) are saved only in your browser on your device (local storage). We do not receive them and cannot see them.

  • We do not read your email, bank account, or online accounts.
  • We do not run ads, analytics, or tracking cookies in the app.
  • If you clear your browser data or change phones, your entries are lost unless you used Backup to save a file. Keep your own backups.

3. Services that process data

  • Hosting (GitHub Pages). The app is delivered through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when you load the app, under its own privacy policy.
  • Phone alerts (ntfy.sh), optional. If you turn on "Alerts when the app is closed", TrialGuard sends reminder messages to the third-party service ntfy.sh. Each message can contain the service name, amount, currency and timing of the reminder. A random topic name acts as your private channel: keep it secret and do not share it. ntfy.sh may keep messages for a limited time under its own policy. You can turn alerts off in the app at any time; already queued alerts can be stopped by unsubscribing from the topic in the ntfy app.
  • Payments (Stripe), optional. The paid unlock is processed by Stripe and its Managed Payments / Link services. Stripe handles your payment details, so we never see or store your card number. We may receive purchase information such as your email address, the amount, and the payment status. Stripe's privacy policy applies to that processing.
  • Notifications. If you allow browser notifications, reminders are shown by your device. This permission can be changed in your browser settings.
  • Offline use. The app saves its own files in your browser so it can open without a connection.

4. Your choices and rights

You can delete your entries inside the app, turn off phone alerts, or clear the site data in your browser to remove everything stored on your device. Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move personal data we hold, to object to processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because the app stores your entries only on your device, the personal data we may hold is limited to purchase information. To make a request, email us at msouai96@gmail.com.

TrialGuard is not directed at children under 16.

5. Terms of use

  • What TrialGuard does. It records dates and amounts you enter and shows reminders. It does not cancel anything with a provider. The "I cancelled with provider" button only records your own decision. You must cancel directly with the service.
  • No guarantee of reminders. Reminders depend on your device, browser settings, internet connection, battery settings and third-party services (such as ntfy.sh). Phone alerts can be scheduled only a few days ahead, so open the app regularly. Do not rely on TrialGuard as your only way to track payments.
  • Not financial or legal advice. Monthly estimates are simple calculations from what you enter and may not match your actual charges.
  • Free plan and unlock. The free plan tracks up to 5 entries. A one-time payment unlocks unlimited entries on the device where you complete the purchase. It is not a subscription and does not renew. The unlock is saved on your device; if you lose your data, contact us with your payment receipt and we will try to help.
  • Refunds. Refund requests are handled according to applicable consumer law and the payment provider's rules. Contact us at msouai96@gmail.com with your receipt.
  • Acceptable use. Do not misuse the app, try to bypass the purchase, or interfere with its operation.
  • Liability. TrialGuard is provided "as is". To the extent allowed by law, we are not responsible for missed reminders, lost data, or charges you incur from third-party services. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have by law that cannot be limited.
  • Changes. We may update this page. The date at the top shows the latest version.
  • Governing law. These terms are governed by the laws of Greece, without affecting any mandatory consumer rights in your country of residence.
